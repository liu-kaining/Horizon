## [First Combined Pig Liver-Kidney Xenotransplant in Human Achieved by Chinese Team](https://www.ithome.com/0/959/079.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A Chinese medical team successfully performed the first human case of combined xenotransplantation of a gene-edited pig's entire liver and two kidneys into a brain-dead human recipient, achieving functional viability for approximately 106 hours. This milestone directly addresses the global organ shortage crisis and represents a major leap forward for multi-organ xenotransplantation, demonstrating that multiple gene-edited pig organs can function in a human body and paving the way for future clinical trials. The team used advanced multi-omics analysis (scRNA-seq, proteomics, metabolomics) to create a detailed immune-metabolic profile, identifying specific neutrophil and signaling pathways as key to xenograft response. The study acknowledges limitations including the single case, short observation window, and the need to address long-term coagulation stability and chronic rejection.

**Background**: Xenotransplantation involves transplanting organs from one species to another, with gene-edited pigs being the most promising source due to their physiological similarity to humans. Gene editing strategies, such as knocking out pig genes that trigger hyperacute rejection and inserting human genes to improve compatibility, are critical for reducing immune barriers. Multi-omics integration is a cutting-edge approach used to comprehensively analyze the complex biological interactions and responses at the molecular level following transplantation.
