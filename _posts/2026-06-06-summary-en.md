## [OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Lockdown Mode to Block Data Exfiltration](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/5/openai-help-lockdown-mode/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has launched Lockdown Mode for ChatGPT, a security feature now rolling out to eligible personal and business accounts that limits outbound network requests to prevent data exfiltration from prompt injection attacks. This feature directly addresses the critical 'Lethal Trifecta' of AI security by cutting off the data exfiltration vector, which is considered the easiest leg to restrict without significantly reducing system utility, thereby providing a deterministic and more robust defense against a major attack surface. Lockdown Mode does not prevent prompt injections from appearing in the content ChatGPT processes, such as in cached web content or uploaded files, meaning the attacks can still affect response behavior or accuracy, but it aims to block the final exfiltration step.

rss · Simon Willison · Jun 5, 23:56

**Background**: Prompt injection is an attack where malicious instructions are embedded into content (like web pages or documents) to mislead an AI model. The 'Lethal Trifecta' in AI security describes the dangerous combination of an LLM having access to private data, being exposed to untrusted content, and having a mechanism to transmit stolen data to an attacker. Data exfiltration refers to the unauthorized transfer of sensitive data out of a system.
