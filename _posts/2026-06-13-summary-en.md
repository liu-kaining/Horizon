This approach was published in Nature and demonstrated effectiveness against 'undruggable' cancers like ovarian and pancreatic cancer. This technique offers a potential new strategy for targeting cancers that currently lack effective treatments, including those with common tumor suppressor mutations found in up to 90% of certain hard-to-treat cancers. It could expand the scope of CRISPR-based therapies beyond traditional gene editing to selective cell killing based on gene expression patterns. The system uses Cas12a2, which upon detecting a target RNA sequence, unleashes indiscriminate nuclease activity that shreds chromatin throughout the cell, leading to cell death. This platform represents a major leap in AI hardware for building large-scale agentic AI systems, and the company's projection of at least $1 trillion in combined sales from its Blackwell and Rubin series by 2027 underscores the anticipated massive growth and investment in AI infrastructure. The Vera CPU is claimed to be twice as efficient and 50% faster than traditional rack-level CPUs, with products from partners starting in the second half of the year; the full Vera Rubin NVL72 rack-scale supercomputer integrates 72 Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs.

**Background**: The Vera Rubin platform is NVIDIA's successor to its Blackwell architecture, named after the astronomer Vera Rubin, and is designed for the next wave of AI factories. It leverages advanced TSMC 3nm manufacturing and HBM4 memory. The Blackwell architecture is NVIDIA's current high-performance GPU microarchitecture that succeeded Hopper.
