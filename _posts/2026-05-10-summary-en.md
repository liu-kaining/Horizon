## [Microsoft Research Introduces PCT Scheduler for Finding Concurrency Bugs](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/asplos277-pct.pdf) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Microsoft Research presented the Probabilistic Concurrency Testing (PCT) scheduler, a randomized algorithm that provides mathematical guarantees for finding concurrency bugs. In a single run with n threads and k steps, it detects a bug of depth d with probability at least 1/(n*k^(d-1)). This work provides a principled and efficient alternative to ad-hoc stress testing for concurrency bugs, offering predictable reliability in bug discovery for critical software systems. It bridges the gap between exhaustive systematic testing (which is often infeasible) and purely random testing (which lacks guarantees). The scheduler's key innovation is using randomization sparingly and in a disciplined manner to provide efficient probabilistic guarantees. Repeated independent runs can increase the probability of finding bugs to any desired level of certainty.

rss · Lobsters · May 9, 04:27

**Background**: Concurrency bugs are notoriously difficult to detect and reproduce because they depend on specific, often rare, thread interleaving schedules. Traditional stress testing is unreliable, while exhaustive systematic testing (exploring all possible schedules) suffers from severe state space explosion. The concept of "bug depth" (d) is used to parameterize the difficulty of exposing a bug, representing the minimum number of scheduling constraints required.
