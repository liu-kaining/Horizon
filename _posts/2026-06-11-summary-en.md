## [JUNO's first physics result published on Nature cover](https://www.ithome.com/0/962/713.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The Jiangmen Underground Neutrino Observatory (JUNO) published its first physics result as a Nature cover article, achieving high-precision measurement of two key neutrino oscillation parameters θ12 and Δm²21 with a precision improvement of 1.6 times over previous experiments. This result marks the beginning of a new era of precision neutrino oscillation measurements, establishing JUNO's critical role in future experiments to determine neutrino mass ordering and advancing our understanding of fundamental particle physics. The analysis used 59 days of effective data collected from August 26 to November 2, 2025, and JUNO's detector is located 700 meters underground with a 20-kiloton liquid scintillator central detector.

rss · IT HOME · Jun 10, 23:56

**Background**: Neutrino oscillation is a quantum phenomenon where neutrinos change between different flavors (electron, muon, tau) as they travel, and the parameters θ12 and Δm²21 are key quantities describing this mixing in the Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) framework. Determining the neutrino mass ordering—whether the lightest neutrino mass state corresponds to the electron flavor or not—is a major goal in neutrino physics, which JUNO aims to address.
